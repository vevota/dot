Look for clues in your life pertaining to The Creator, such as your dreams, visions, and His designs. Pray to Him. Give Him your life, and in exchange, He will give you an eternity in The Celestial Realm. As the incoming calamity begins, The Celestial Gateway will cease to exist. You are lucky to gain this knowledge before it's too late. The Creator will continue watching over all mortals until the eradication arrives.
